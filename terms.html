3. Description of Services

Cryptaverse (formerly Aleph Vault) is a privacy-first encrypted application for Android providing local encrypted storage of notes, passwords, cryptographic key pairs and API keys; post-quantum encrypted messaging; encrypted file transfer and storage; and a public publishing surface where you may choose to publish blocks and stacks that anyone can read.

EGAF (Entropy-Gated AI Framework) is an open AI governance protocol developed by JC Labs R&D, defining what an AI assistant is permitted to do before it runs, so that its behaviour is bounded, reproducible and auditable.

Abe (formerly the Gaffer) is our AI assistant, with EGAF governance built into it. Both are currently offline while being rebuilt and are not available.

5. Public and Private Content

Cryptaverse separates private content from content you publish. Your vault, private profile screen, messages and files are encrypted on your device and cannot be read by JC Laboratories. Blocks and stacks you publish to the public surface, together with any media attached to them and any public profile you create, are stored unencrypted so they can be served to others.

Publishing is always a deliberate action. You are solely responsible for what you choose to publish, and you should assume anything you publish may be copied, indexed or retained by third parties beyond our control. Unpublishing removes content from our servers; it cannot retrieve copies others have already made.

6. Account Password and Vault Password

Account password — used to sign in to your JC Laboratories account. Password resets are handled by email-delivered, time-limited reset tokens. Your email address is processed for authentication and reset purposes but is not stored as plaintext; we store a keyed cryptographic representation (HMAC-SHA256) for account lookup, which cannot be reversed.

Vault password — no recovery on standard tiers. Your vault password derives your encryption key locally on your device. JC Laboratories does not store, transmit, or have any access to your vault password or its derived key. If you forget it on Free, Individual or Family tiers, your encrypted vault data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. There is no reset, no recovery code and no backdoor. This is intentional and is a direct consequence of the security model.

You acknowledge and accept this limitation by using standard tiers. Export your vault regularly and store the backup securely.

7. Vault Export and Import

Available on Individual tier and above. Export creates an encrypted backup file protected by an export password you choose at the time of export, separate from and independent of your vault password. It is not stored by JC Laboratories.

Your export password cannot be recovered. If you lose it, the backup file cannot be decrypted. Store it separately from the backup file itself. You are solely responsible for the security of exported files once they leave the app.

8. Safety Deposit Box — Cloud Backup

Family, Business and Enterprise subscribers may enable Safety Deposit Box, our encrypted cloud backup. Vault data is encrypted on-device using AES-256-GCM before upload. JC Laboratories stores only the encrypted ciphertext and cannot decrypt it. On Free and Individual tiers, vault data never leaves your device. Encrypted blobs are retained while the subscription is active and deleted 30 days after cancellation.
