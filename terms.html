5. What Is Encrypted, and What Is Not

You decide what stays private and what becomes public. Nothing moves between those states on its own. Content in Cryptaverse sits in one of three states:

Private — encrypted, delivered only to people you name. Your vault, your profile screen, your posts, private groups, messages, and Drive folders you have not shared. These are encrypted on your device before they reach us. We hold ciphertext and the routing information needed to deliver it. We cannot read any of it, and neither can anyone you have not sent it to.

Shared by link — encrypted, with the key released to whoever redeems the link. Drive folders you share. The files stay encrypted; redeeming a link performs a key exchange that delivers the folder key to that person. If you mark a folder link as searchable, its name becomes discoverable — the files inside remain encrypted and unreadable to us. Anyone holding the link can obtain access, so treat a link as the key it is.

Public — stored unencrypted, because it is meant to be read. Blocks and stacks you publish and any media attached to them, a public profile if you create one, and the name, description and rules of a group you make public. This content is served to anyone who asks for it, and is readable by us. A public group's messages are not in this category — the group's listing is public, its contents are not.

Publishing and sharing are always deliberate actions you take. You are solely responsible for what you make public or share by link, and you should assume anything in either state may be copied, indexed or retained by third parties beyond our control. Unpublishing removes content from our servers and revoking a link prevents further redemptions; neither can retrieve copies already made, or revoke access from someone who has already redeemed a link.

6. Account Password and Vault Password

Account password — used to sign in to your JC Laboratories account. Password resets are handled by email-delivered, time-limited reset tokens. Your email address is processed for authentication and reset purposes but is not stored as plaintext; we store a keyed cryptographic representation for account lookup, which cannot be reversed.

Vault password — no recovery. Your vault password derives your encryption key locally on your device. JC Laboratories does not store, transmit, or have any access to your vault password or its derived key. If you forget it, your encrypted vault data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. There is no reset, no recovery code and no backdoor. This is intentional and is a direct consequence of the security model: a vault we could open for you is a vault we could be compelled to open for someone else.

You acknowledge and accept this limitation by using the Services. Export your vault regularly and store the backup somewhere safe.

7. Vault Export and Import

Available on every tier, including Free. Export creates an encrypted backup file protected by an export password you choose at the time of export, separate from and independent of your vault password. It is not stored by JC Laboratories.

Your export password cannot be recovered. If you lose it, the backup file cannot be decrypted. Store it separately from the backup file itself. You are solely responsible for the security of exported files once they leave the app.

8. Safety Deposit Box — Cloud Backup

Personal and Pro subscribers may enable Safety Deposit Box, our encrypted cloud backup. Vault data is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before upload. JC Laboratories stores only the encrypted ciphertext and cannot decrypt it. On the Free tier, vault data never leaves your device. Encrypted backups count against your storage allowance, are retained while the subscription is active, and are deleted 30 days after cancellation.
